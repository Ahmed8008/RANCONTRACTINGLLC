Who We Are

A Trusted Partner for Technical Excellence.

RAN Contracting & Technical Services LLC is a leading provider of General Contracting, Power & Energy, Oil & Petrochemical, and Telecommunication solutions in the UAE. Since our establishment in 2022, we have built a reputation for delivering high-quality, reliable, and innovative services across multiple industries.

We specialize in turnkey contracting, technical services, equipment rental, and project management, combining industry expertise with advanced technology to ensure projects are completed efficiently, safely, and to the highest standards.

Our mission is to provide client-focused solutions that drive success, while our vision is to become the most trusted and innovative service provider in the Gulf region.

At RAN Contracting, we believe in building strong, lasting partnerships with our clients based on transparency, professionalism, and performance.
